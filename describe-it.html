    Grammar Exercises

    B2 · C1
    Passive voice is used when the action is more important than who does it, or when we don't know who did it.
    Form: subject + be (correct tense) + past participle
    Examples: The photo was taken at sunrise. The city has been transformed. The report will be published next week.
    💡 Grammar hint — passive forms
    was/were + pp
    has/have been + pp
    is/are being + pp
    will be + pp
    had been + pp
    Conditional sentences — choose the correct type:
    Zero: If + present simple, present simple (general truths)
    First: If + present simple, will + infinitive (real/possible future)
    Second: If + past simple, would + infinitive (unlikely/hypothetical present)
    Third: If + past perfect, would have + past participle (impossible past)
    Mixed: If + past perfect, would + infinitive (past cause → present result)
    I wish / If only express regret or desire.
    • Wish + past simple → present situation you want to change: I wish I lived near the beach.
    • Wish + past perfect → past regret: I wish I had taken more photos.
    • Wish + would → annoying habit you want to change: I wish he would stop blaring his horn.
    If only has the same grammar but is more emphatic.
    Mixed tenses — choose or write the correct tense form. Think carefully about: is the action finished or ongoing? Is it past, present, or future? Is it a habit, a single event, or something happening at this exact moment?
    Adjectives + prepositions are fixed combinations — you must learn them as collocations.
    Common ones: amazed at/by, aware of, responsible for, fond of, similar to, different from, good at, interested in, frightened of, proud of, devoted to, fed up with
