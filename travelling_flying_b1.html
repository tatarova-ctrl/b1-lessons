Almost Missed My Flight!

★ Tom, 28 · from his travel blog

So, last summer I flew to Barcelona with my girlfriend, and honestly, it was one of those trips where everything that could go wrong, did go wrong — but we still had an amazing time.

It all started when we booked our flights pretty last minute. We got a nice deal with a low-cost airline, but of course, that meant no free food and a tiny baggage allowance. My girlfriend, Kate, spent about three hours trying to squeeze everything into her carry-on. I kept telling her, "Just pay for a checked bag!" but she wasn't having any of it.

On the day of the flight, we left the house way too late. Traffic was a nightmare, and we only got to the airport about an hour before take-off. I was panicking. We ran to the check-in desk, dropped off our bags, and then had to go through security. Of course, Kate had forgotten to take the water out of her bag, so they stopped her. Classic.

Then came the worst part — we looked up at the departures board, and our gate was at the other end of the terminal. We basically sprinted. When we finally got to the gate, they were boarding the last passengers. The flight attendant gave us this look, like, "seriously?" But she smiled and said:

"Cutting it a bit close, guys. Welcome aboard."

The flight itself was fine, although we had a bumpy ride for about twenty minutes because of some turbulence. Kate hates flying, so she was gripping my hand like crazy. The pilot came on and said, "fasten your seatbelts" and warned us about bad weather. Not exactly what you want to hear.

When we landed in Barcelona, there was another surprise waiting for us. We went to pick up our luggage, and Kate's suitcase didn't come out. We stood there forever watching the same bags go round and round on the belt. In the end, we had to report it as lost luggage. It showed up two days later at our hotel, but by then Kate had already bought a whole new outfit!

Honestly, next time I'm flying, I'm leaving the house five hours early. No joke. But yeah — despite all that, Spain was incredible. I'd do it all again tomorrow.

📝Grammar: 3 tenses in action

Mini quick review first, then an exercise mixing all three tenses — in a travel context, of course.

Quick reminder — when to use each

  • Present Simple → habits, routines, facts. I usually fly with Ryanair.
  • Present Continuous → happening right now or around now. We're boarding in five minutes.
  • Past Simple → finished actions in the past. We landed at 8 p.m. yesterday.
